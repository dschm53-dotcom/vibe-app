Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. You can request full deletion of your Vibemap account and all associated data using the options below.

⚠️

This action is permanent and irreversible. Deleting your account will remove all your posts, profile data, friend connections, and any other data associated with your account. This cannot be undone.

📱 Option 1 – Delete from the App

The easiest way to delete your account is directly within the Vibemap app:

  1. Open the Vibemap app on your device
  2. Tap your Profile icon in the bottom navigation bar
  3. Tap the Settings (⚙️) icon in the top right corner
  4. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

or

Option 2 – Request via Email

If you are unable to access the app, you can email us directly. Please include your registered email address in your request and we will delete your account within 7 business days.

📋 What Data Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • ✅ Your account profile (name, email, photo)
  • ✅ All posts and vibes you created
  • ✅ Your friend connections and social data
  • ✅ Push notification tokens
  • ✅ Any other personal data associated with your account
