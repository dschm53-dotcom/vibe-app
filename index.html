How It Works

Three steps to own your city

Go places, post your vibe, and let the community decide who reigns.

01
📍

Check In & Post

Visit a place, capture the moment with a photo, and tag your location. Every post automatically enters you into that place's weekly competition.

02
🗳️

Vote & Compete

The community votes on photos within a rolling 7-day window. The more votes your photos get, the higher you climb on the leaderboard.

03
👑

Hold the Territory

Every Wednesday at 12pm, the new holder is crowned. Get the keys to the place — pin posts, set the vibe, and leave your mark.

Place Ownership

A community-driven system
for every location

Parks, cafes, beaches, stadiums — every location has a leaderboard. Defend your hold, or rise up and take it.

📌

Pin Your Photos

Your best photos pinned front and centre on the place page — seen by everyone who visits.

✏️

Holder's Note

Post a 160-character message visible to every visitor. A recommendation, a challenge, or whatever you want.

🎨

Theme the Page

Choose an accent colour that themes the entire place profile while you hold it. Unmistakably yours.

⚠️

Threat Alerts

Get notified the moment a challenger reaches 70% of your vote count — time to defend.

Features

Everything you need to vibe harder

See the emotional pulse of your city in real time. Every check-in adds to a live heatmap of moods across locations.
